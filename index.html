001 About

Composed, not linear.

My journey in storytelling was never linear; it was composed. From the quiet precision of playing the piano and cello in opera halls to the raw expression of classical theatre and voice acting, art has always been my native language. Before I directed frames, I studied movement, melody, and voice. Theater taught me human truth; the orchestra taught me harmony; filmmaking gave those elements a home. My work across borders - from Egypt and KSA to France and Germany - has given me a kaleidoscope of vision. Every concept I create is guided by a singular question: How many layers of truth can we uncover when we view a story from every angle?
